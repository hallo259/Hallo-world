Wallet setup

  1. Install the official app or extension of a Solana wallet (such as Phantom or Solflare).
  2. Create a new wallet and write your seed phrase on paper, stored somewhere safe. Never put it in a screenshot or a chat.
  3. Send SOL to your wallet address from an exchange.
  4. Keep a little extra SOL beyond your buy amount to cover fees.

Buy on Pump.fun

Pump.fun is a meme coin launchpad on Solana where each coin has its own page.

  1. Open Pump.fun from your bookmark or the official link.
  2. Connect your Solana wallet.
  3. Paste the official contract address of $HALLO into the search.
  4. When the coin's page opens, check that the address matches the one on this site.
  5. Enter your SOL amount, press Buy and confirm the transaction in your wallet.

Buy on Fomo

Fomo is a self-custodial mobile trading app that also has a social feed.

  1. Install the Fomo app only from the official app store or official link.
  2. Create a new wallet or import your own.
  3. Add SOL to your wallet.
  4. Paste the official contract address of $HALLO into the search.
  5. Once the address matches, enter your amount and confirm the buy.

Buy on Axiom Pro

Axiom is a fast trading terminal built for Solana meme coins.

  1. Open Axiom from your bookmark or the official link.
  2. Connect your wallet or create a new one there.
  3. Keep SOL in your wallet.
  4. Paste the official contract address of $HALLO into the search.
  5. Set your amount and slippage (slippage is how much the price is allowed to move), then buy.

Heads up: many fake sites and "access code" posts circulate under the name "Axiom Pro". Always go through the official link.

All three platforms change their design from time to time. Follow what you see on your screen.

Safety

  • Use only the official contract address. Fake tokens with the same name are common.
  • Never give your seed phrase or private key to anyone: not support, not admins, not DMs.
  • Don't connect your wallet to unknown links.
  • Meme coins are very risky. Only put in what you can afford to lose.
